We use your personal information to:

We do not sell your data to third parties. We may share your data with trusted partners, such as hosting providers or payment processors, to facilitate our services. We may also share data to comply with legal obligations or enforce our terms.

We use cookies and similar technologies to track user behavior on our website and improve our services. You may disable cookies via your browser settings, but this may impact your experience on our site.

US Residents (CCPA): You have the right to request the categories of data we collect, opt out of data sales, and request deletion of your data. Contact us at contact@deltasystem.in to exercise these rights.
India Residents: You have the right to access and correct your personal data, and we will notify you of data breaches under applicable laws.

Data Security
We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

Data Retention
We retain your personal data for as long as it is necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and you are encouraged to review it regularly.

For any privacy-related queries, contact us at contact@deltasystem.in.
